04 / A LETTER

What I Never Say Out Loud.

Dear [HER NAME], I have wanted to say this properly for a long time, and since I am much better at building websites than at saying emotional things out loud, here we are. Thank you. For everything, big and small, including the things you probably forgot you ever did for me.

You have this way of making people feel like they matter. When I talk, you listen. When I am wrong, you tell me gently. When I am right, you hype me up like I just won an award. Not many people give that kind of energy, and I never take it for granted.

I think about how easy it would have been for you to be a friend only in the good moments. But you stayed through the awkward ones, the stressful ones and the completely random ones too. That is what real friendship looks like, and you have set the bar so high that I am now unbearably picky about everyone else.

You are also one of the funniest people I know. I do not mean in a loud, look-at-me way. I mean the kind of funny where one comment from you has me laughing for ten minutes straight. Some of my best memories are just the two of us being ridiculous for no reason at all.

And you are so much stronger than you give yourself credit for. I have watched you deal with hard things and keep going, keep smiling and keep caring about other people while you did it. If you ever doubt yourself, I want you to remember that I see it, and wallahi that it is incredible.

You make me want to be a better friend. Every time you check in, every time you remember something small that I said weeks ago, I think: I want to be that person for her too. I hope I am, even a little.

So this is my very dramatic, very over-engineered way of saying: you matter, you are appreciated, and you are not getting rid of me. Thank you for being exactly who you are.

With all my gratitude,
Ayub ♥
